You · Aug 20
I've had some nausea the last two weeks, mostly the day after my injection. It's not terrible but it's noticeable. Is this something I should worry about, or does it settle down?
Dr. Rhee · Aug 21
That level of nausea is expected at this stage — it usually shows up in the first few weeks after a dose increase and eases as your body adjusts. Since yours is mild and only lasting a day, I'd stay the course.

Two things that help: eat smaller meals with more protein on injection day, and try moving your injection to the evening so you sleep through the worst of it.

If it becomes moderate or starts lasting more than two days, message me and we'll consider holding at this dose instead of increasing in September.
Dr. Rhee · Aug 21
